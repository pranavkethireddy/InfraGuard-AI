# Model card: drive-failure-v1 (0.1.0)

Generated by `scripts/train_drive.py`. Every number below is computed by the training script.

- **Task:** Backblaze drive records failure within 30 days of the observation day. Horizon 720 h.
- **Data:** real Backblaze data-center hard drives, https://f001.backblazeb2.com/file/Backblaze-Hard-Drive-Data/data_Q1_2025.zip (Backblaze Drive Stats; free to use with attribution to Backblaze).
  203500 sampled drive-days, 7242 drives, 1061 failed in the quarter.
- **Features:** reallocated_sectors, reported_uncorrectable, command_timeout, pending_sectors, offline_uncorrectable, crc_errors, power_on_hours, temperature_c (SMART raw values; missing values allowed).
- **Model:** logreg_C0.1, chosen on validation PR-AUC.
- **Split:** split by drive serial_number, stratified by 'failed in quarter', 60/20/20, fixed seed.

## Held-out test (fleet-reweighted)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|

| ROC-AUC | 0.8308 |

| Recall @ high | 0.3311 |

| High threshold | 0.0203 |
| Medium threshold | 0.0102 |

Non-failing drives were sampled at neg_drive_rate; metrics and confusion-matrix counts are reweighted by 1/neg_drive_rate to estimate the real fleet. n and positives are sampled row counts.

## Limitations

- One quarter of one fleet (Backblaze, Q1 2025); other vendors, workloads and SMART encodings may differ.
- Scores are not calibrated probabilities.
- Sudden failures with no SMART warning cannot be predicted from these features.
